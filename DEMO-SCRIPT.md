# Two-Minute Judge Demo Script

Use the normal Build 50 URL for the recording. Complete onboarding beforehand, select English, close unrelated tabs and begin on `#home`. Judge Mode is useful for live evaluation, but keep its reviewer controls out of the recorded citizen story.

## 0:00–1:00 — One citizen, one complete journey

### 0:00–0:10 — Hook

**Screen:** Home, with Ravi's urgent licence action visible.

> “India does not have a transport digitisation problem. It has a last-mile understanding problem. A service may be online, but citizens still may not know what to do next.”

### 0:10–0:22 — Make it human

**Screen:** Point to **28 days left** and the renewal action.

> “Meet Ravi. He has two vehicles, several documents and one urgent personal deadline. Parivahan Sarathi begins with the only question that matters: what should Ravi do next?”

### 0:22–0:40 — Show the transformation

**Action:** Select **Start renewal guide**. Pause on the four steps, state caveat and official source.

> “Instead of making Ravi understand departments and forms, Sarathi turns the deadline into four plain-language steps—what to prepare, how long it may take and where the official handoff begins.”

### 0:40–0:53 — Close the loop

**Action:** Open **Documents → Needs attention → Driving Licence**. Point to **Save offline**, **Share** and the source date.

> “The affected licence is already waiting for him. He can keep an offline snapshot, share it deliberately and verify the source before acting.”

### 0:53–1:00 — Inclusion

**Action:** Switch to Hindi.

> “And the same next action remains understandable in Hindi, on the phone Ravi already uses.”

## 1:00–2:00 — Why this product is different

### 1:00–1:17 — Product insight

**Action:** Switch back to English and return Home.

> “We learned that another service directory was not the answer. So we designed a person-centred transport guardian across licences, vehicles, documents and applications—with urgency before navigation.”

### 1:17–1:36 — Responsible AI moment

**Action:** Open **Services → International Driving Permit**. Pause on the Form Agent and its green safety boundary.

> “For form-heavy services, the Form Agent organises reusable profile data, finds missing information and prepares a draft. But it never handles CAPTCHA, OTP, declarations, payment or final submission. AI prepares; the citizen authorises.”

### 1:36–1:50 — Build credibility

**Screen:** Keep the safety boundary or official handoff visible.

> “This prototype uses synthetic data and deterministic demonstrations, so no government record or payment is touched. We used OpenAI Codex throughout design, implementation, testing and responsible-AI evaluation—and documented every production boundary.”

### 1:50–2:00 — Memorable close

**Action:** Return Home and hold on Ravi's single urgent action.

> “Parivahan Sarathi does not replace India's transport portals. It removes the confusion before them—because digital public infrastructure succeeds only when citizens can complete the journey with confidence.”

## Delivery notes

- Target **1:50–1:58**; never exceed two minutes.
- Speak calmly at roughly **135–145 words per minute**. Pause after the hook and final line.
- Use browser zoom around **110–125%** for a 1080p recording.
- Keep the pointer still while speaking; move only when performing the next action.
- Show one complete citizen story, not every feature.
- Use captions, a quiet room and no visible notifications.
- End on the product—not code, a terminal or documentation.
- Do not claim live government data, a live AI model, a real payment or measured population impact.

## Emergency 30-second version

> “A service being online does not mean a citizen knows what to do. Parivahan Sarathi finds Ravi's next urgent transport task, explains it in plain language, keeps his documents available offline and hands him safely to the correct official service. For form-heavy journeys, AI prepares a reviewable draft—but Ravi retains CAPTCHA, OTP, payment and submission. It is bilingual, mobile-first and built with explicit mock boundaries. Sarathi does not replace the portal; it removes the confusion before it.”
