# Maharashtra Pilot Plan

## Objective

Test whether Parivahan Sarathi helps citizens understand and prepare for transport tasks without weakening consent, privacy or official-portal control.

## Eight-week sequence

1. **Weeks 1–2 — safety and content review:** validate service copy with an RTO-domain reviewer, legal/privacy reviewer and Hindi/Marathi language reviewers. Replace synthetic links only with approved endpoints.
2. **Weeks 3–4 — moderated usability:** 12–18 consenting adults across low, medium and high digital confidence. Use synthetic records only.
3. **Weeks 5–6 — assisted kiosk pilot:** 50–100 sessions at one partner help centre. Staff can explain the study but must not complete identity, CAPTCHA, OTP, signature or payment for participants.
4. **Weeks 7–8 — limited opt-in beta:** invite up to 250 participants; provide visible feedback and human escalation; monitor only consented, minimised events.

## Inclusion targets

Include first-time portal users, older adults, two-wheeler and multi-vehicle households, users of English/Hindi/Marathi plus at least one additional Indian language, screen-reader users, and participants on low-cost Android devices or constrained connectivity.

## Measures

- Task completion and wrong turns
- Time to correct official handoff
- Comprehension of deadlines and required evidence
- Understanding of mock/official boundaries
- Accessibility defects and language misunderstandings
- Safety events: credential entry, incorrect jurisdiction, duplicate-payment risk
- Optional satisfaction score, never used alone

## Stop conditions

Pause the pilot on any real-data exposure, misleading claim of government affiliation, unsafe payment direction, repeat critical hallucination, inaccessible blocker, or participant inability to withdraw/delete pilot data.

## Governance

Publish a data dictionary, retention window, consent text, incident owner and change log before recruitment. Do not connect production systems until authorized data access, security assessment, DPIA/privacy review and support ownership are in place.
