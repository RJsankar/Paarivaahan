# Submission Summary

**Word count: 193**

Indian transport services are spread across unfamiliar portals, forms, abbreviations and state-specific processes. Citizens often discover an expiring licence, missing document or challan only when it becomes urgent—and still do not know what to do next.

Parivahan Sarathi is a multilingual, mobile-first transport guardian. It brings a person's licence, vehicles, documents, deadlines and applications into one understandable workspace. Instead of making citizens navigate a service directory, Sarathi identifies the next important action, explains why it matters, prepares the required information, and hands the citizen safely to the correct official service.

The working prototype demonstrates English and Hindi interfaces; proactive licence alerts; multi-vehicle records; offline document snapshots and sharing; plain-language document guidance; application tracking; a controlled challan-payment simulation; and guided services. Its International Driving Permit Form Agent reuses verified synthetic profile information, checks missing evidence and prepares a reviewable draft while leaving CAPTCHA, OTP, declarations, payment and final submission with the citizen.

The prototype uses synthetic records and simulated AI, payment, notification and government integrations. It never accesses a live government system and clearly identifies these boundaries. A production version would use consent, approved APIs, versioned official sources, deterministic workflows and receipt reconciliation.
