## Main journey for reviewers

1. Open Home and find Ravi's driving-licence deadline.
2. Select **Renew licence** or open **Alerts → Guide me**.
3. Review the plain-language renewal steps and official source.
4. Open **Documents → Needs attention → Driving Licence** to inspect the saved record and actions.
5. Switch between English and Hindi, then resize to a mobile viewport.

Optional depth: open **Services → International Driving Permit** to see the citizen-controlled Form Agent prepare a synthetic draft without submitting to a government system.

Reviewer shortcut: append `?judge=1#home` to load a deterministic synthetic state with direct buttons for the licence journey, document wallet and Form Agent.

## Required disclosure

Parivahan Sarathi is an independent hackathon prototype and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Government of India. Citizen records, document extraction, AI responses, notifications, government integrations, payments, receipts, and application status are synthetic or simulated. No live government system is accessed. Production would require approved integrations, identity and consent controls, regulatory review, security assessment, and receipt reconciliation.
