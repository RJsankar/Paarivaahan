## Recommended five-session protocol

Recruit adults who use a smartphone but are not familiar with the Parivahan portal. With consent, ask each participant to:

1. Explain what they think the product does after ten seconds.
2. Find the next task Ravi should complete.
3. Work out how to renew the driving licence.
4. Find and share the relevant document.
5. Explain what was real, what was mocked and whether the product is official.

Do not teach the interface during the task. Record completion, time, wrong turns and participant language. Never enter real identity or transport data.
